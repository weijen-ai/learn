How a guide is made

1 · Source

Start from real material

A learner shares a chapter, a page, or their own notes. The source stays with the learner; it is not uploaded here.

2 · Guide

Explain, then practise

Objectives, a step-by-step explanation, flashcards, and a short quiz with immediate feedback.

3 · Publish

Static and simple

Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No accounts, no tracking, works on a phone.

Content and copyright

Original material only. Guides are AI-assisted summaries, explanations, and exercises written for learning. They cite their sources but do not reproduce textbook scans, photographs of pages, or long verbatim passages. If something here should not be public, please open an issue on the repository.
