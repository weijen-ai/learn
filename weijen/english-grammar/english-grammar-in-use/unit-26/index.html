Can, could & (be) able to

分清「現在能力」、「過去的一般能力」與「某次成功做到」三種說法。

2026-09-06 AI-assisted study guide, grounded in: English Grammar in Use, Unit 26 〈Can, could and (be) able to〉 · pp. 52–53

Learning objectives

  • 用 can / can't 表示現在的能力或可能
  • 用 could / couldn't 談過去的一般能力
  • 在特定的成功事件用 was/were able to 或 managed to
  • 在完成式、情態動詞後或不定詞裡改用 (be) able to

Explanation, step by step

一張決策地圖

先問自己：這句話在講現在、過去的一般能力，還是某一次成功做到？

  • 現在／一般情況：can + 原形動詞。I can swim.（我會游泳。）
  • 過去的一般能力：could + 原形動詞。When I was six, I could read.（六歲時我會閱讀。）
  • 某一次成功做到：was/were able to 或 managed to + 原形動詞。After two calls, we managed to reach her.
1｜Can 的限制：需要其他時態時改用 (be) able to

can 只有現在式 can 與過去式 could。當句子需要完成式、放在情態動詞後面，或需要不定詞時，就用 be able to。

  • I can't sleep tonight.
  • I haven't been able to sleep recently.（完成式）
  • She might be able to join us.（情態動詞後）
  • You must be able to explain it clearly.

記憶法：看到 have、might、must 這類結構，先問自己「can 能不能放在這裡？」不能時多半用 (be) able to。

2｜Could：過去的普遍能力與感官

could 適合描述一段時間中一貫擁有的能力，也常與感官或理解動詞一起出現：see, hear, smell, taste, feel, remember, understand。

  • My grandfather could speak several languages.
  • From the window, we could see the sea.
  • I couldn't hear the announcement.
3｜最重要的對比：could vs. was able to / managed to

一般能力：Leo was a strong runner. He could run 10 kilometres easily.

一次具體成功：Yesterday, despite the rain, Leo managed to finish the race.

否定句比較寬鬆：couldn't 既可用於一般能力，也可用於特定情況。We tried every route, but we couldn't find the hotel.

為什麼？could 通常表示「有這項能力」。一次事件的肯定句若要表達「最後辦到了」，was/were able to 或 managed to 更精確；但 couldn't 本身就能清楚表示「沒能做到」。

自我檢查：別掉進這些陷阱
  • ❌ Yesterday I could catch the bus. → ✅ Yesterday I managed to catch the bus.（一次具體成功）
  • ❌ I haven't can sleep. → ✅ I haven't been able to sleep.（完成式要用 able to）
  • ❌ We didn't managed to leave. → ✅ We didn't manage to leave.（didn't 後接原形）

Flashcards

Answer in your head, then reveal.

「昨天雖然很塞車，我們還是成功趕上火車。」該用哪個結構？

Show answer

一次具體成功：We managed to catch the train. / We were able to catch the train.

完成式裡要表達「一直沒辦法」，怎麼說？

Show answer

haven't been able to + 原形。例：I haven't been able to sleep recently.

過去長期擁有的能力，用哪個字？

Show answer

could。例：My grandfather could speak several languages.

Although the door was locked, I ______ get inside.

Show answer

managed to / was able to。句子描述一次具體成功，兩者皆合適。
